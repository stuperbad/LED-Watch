## Where the numbers come from (be sceptical)
- The data sheet ranks the boost side (output caps and CBYP first, then CSTOR, then CIN, the boost inductor last) and gives the buck side no ranking. The 1-5 badges merge the two lists, so they are the author's reading, not a data-sheet rank.
- The distance limits (3 mm solar and USB, 1.5 mm MCU and IMU caps, 2 mm crystal block) are rules of thumb, not data-sheet values. The crystal and IMU caps are measured to the chip pin, not to the crystal pad, so they show red although they sit against the crystal.

## Open points
- C54 (output capacitor) is 6.3 mm from its pin, R51 6.5 mm; both are a placement choice, not a routing fault.
- C55, C56, D50, D51 were rotated so the diodes stand toward the middle. C55 / C56 are now 3.7 / 3.8 mm from the charger pins (were 1.4 / 1.8) and C56 reaches the charger by 4.4 mm of copper after CC2 was routed round the south of it (it was 14.5); the BQ21040 data sheet (11.1) wants IN and OUT capacitors as close as possible. D51 is 8.0 mm of copper from the connector (was 4.0). C56 now links straight to D50 pad 1 (1.7 mm); the CC2 track beside it has no margin (exactly 0.1 mm to D50 pad 1, exactly 0.2 mm to the VBUS via hole), fine for the rules but the tightest spot on the board.
- Divider ground returns still reach ground through the plane; the data sheet prefers short traces to a ground pin, separate from the power ground (U4 pin 5 is the nearest).
- 56 pad exits are not along the part axis (accepted by the owner).
- Parity metadata (MPN and datasheet fields, library prefixes) not cleaned up.
