U1 (AVR128DB48) pin map

The chip as it sits on the board: 12 pins per side, coloured by what the net is for. The thin strip between the chip and each pin is its port (PA to PF). The firmware writes a whole port at a time, so what matters is how many ports hold one kind of net and nothing else. Red outlines show what each option changes.

Ports in this option

"Clean" = every used pin on the port is columns, or every one is rows. "Shares with buzzer or I2C" is harmless for the matrix, because the firmware sets and clears only its own bits. A gap in the bit list means the pattern cannot be written as one plain byte.
